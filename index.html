How to play Smarty91
⏱️ 1. Game Cycles & Locking Rules

• Four game duration modes are available: 30s, 1Min, 3Min, and 5Min.

• In each round, when the countdown timer reaches the final 5 seconds, betting is locked to ensure fair calculation and draw processing.

🎨 2. Color Betting Rules & Payouts

• Green: If the drawn number is 1, 3, 7, or 9, payout is 2X (₹100 bet returns ₹196 after 2% fee). If number is 5, payout is 1.5X (₹100 bet returns ₹147).

• Red: If the drawn number is 2, 4, 6, or 8, payout is 2X (₹100 bet returns ₹196 after 2% fee). If number is 0, payout is 1.5X (₹100 bet returns ₹147).

• Violet: If the drawn number is 0 or 5, payout is 4.5X (₹100 bet returns ₹441 after 2% fee).

🔢 3. Number Betting (0 to 9)

• Choose any individual digit from 0 through 9.

• If the winning draw matches your selected number, you receive a massive 9X payout (₹100 bet returns ₹882 after 2% fee).

⚖️ 4. Big / Small Betting

• Big: Covers numbers 5, 6, 7, 8, 9. Payout is 2X (₹100 bet returns ₹196).

• Small: Covers numbers 0, 1, 2, 3, 4. Payout is 2X (₹100 bet returns ₹196).

💎 5. Service Fee & Instant Settlement

• A standard 2% contract fee is deducted from the bet amount on winning payouts (Formula: Bet Amount × Multiplier × 0.98).

• Results and wallet winnings are instantly settled automatically at the end of each round.
